BhagavadGita Chronicles & Silk & Syllable

Two educational YouTube channels run by Dinesh Salunke:

  • BhagavadGita Chronicles - animated short videos that go through the Bhagavad Gita verse by verse.
  • Silk & Syllable (@silkandsyllable) - the stories of words that travelled along trade routes and empires.

The "BhagavadGita Chronicles Poster" app is a private tool the channel owner uses to upload his own finished videos to his own YouTube channels on a schedule. It is not offered to the public.
