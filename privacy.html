Privacy policy

BhagavadGita Chronicles Poster. Last updated 27 November 2026.

This app is a private tool used only by the channel owner, Dinesh Salunke, to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channels (BhagavadGita Chronicles and Silk & Syllable) through the YouTube Data API.

What it accesses

Only the permission to upload videos to the signed-in owner's YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or store viewer data, comments, contacts or any other Google user data.

How data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never sold, shared or sent to anyone else. Nothing is used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube terms

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
